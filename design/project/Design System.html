Design principles

00 · Foundations

Four anchors. Everything we ship should pass all four.

01

Built for regulated environments

Decisions are auditable, signals are explainable, and risk is never hidden behind a glossy chart. If a regulator wouldn't understand it, we redesign it.

02

Data over ornament

Every pixel justifies itself by helping an underwriter decide faster or a credit head trust more. No decorative gradients, no slop iconography.

03

Calm in dense surfaces

Lending screens are information-dense by nature. We earn density with hierarchy, whitespace and typographic restraint — not by cramming.

04

Indian-first, enterprise-ready

Built for ₹, lakhs and crores; for Aadhaar and Account Aggregator; for the regulators we operate under. Localisation is not an afterthought.

Iconography

13 · Components

Line icons at 1.5px stroke, rounded caps. Built on a 22×22 grid for 24px hit area. We don't use filled glyphs in primary UI — only inside violet/ink chips.

Voice & tone

15 · Brand

Confident, declarative, founder-led. We are an infrastructure company talking to other infrastructure builders — not to retail consumers.

Do
  • “The infrastructure is operational today across real lending environments.”
  • “CAM generation: days to under one hour.”
  • “Built for India's regulated lending ecosystem from day one.”
Don't
  • “Revolutionise your lending journey with our cutting-edge AI 🚀”
  • “Unlock the power of intelligent automation, today!”
  • “The world's leading lending platform.”
